How it works

Simple on purpose.

Most to-do apps become another project to manage. Just Do Three strips that away and helps you focus on what actually matters today.

1

Choose your three

Pick only the three tasks that matter most today.

2

3

Finish with momentum

Check off what matters and come back tomorrow with a fresh start.

Philosophy

If everything matters, nothing does.

Just Do Three is built around one idea: your day gets better when you stop pretending you can do everything at once.

Pick three real priorities. Finish what counts. Let the rest wait.

Less organizing. Less guilt. More momentum.
