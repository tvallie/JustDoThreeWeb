Philosophy

Trying to do everything means finishing nothing.

JD3 is built around one idea: your day gets better when you stop pretending you can do everything at once.

Most to-do lists eventually turn into massive backlogs. When you look at them, it's easy to jump between tasks — a little progress here, a little progress there.

But constant switching makes it feel like nothing actually gets done.

JD3 takes a different approach.

① Find your three
② Focus on them
③ Finish them

Less switching. Less overwhelm. More progress.
