Release Notes

What's new in JD3.

Every update to Just Do Three, in one place. Newest first.

Just Do Three keeps getting better, one focused release at a time. Here's what's shipped so far.

Version 3 May 3, 2026 Latest
  • JDT at Work — keep work and personal tasks separate with a quick toggle. Each context has its own backlog, plan, and rollovers.
  • Add to iPhone Calendar — turn any task into a Calendar event or Reminder right from JD3.
  • Export tasks to CSV — pull your backlog (and optionally completed tasks) out of the app for backups or analysis.
  • Scan with Camera & Import from Photos — point your phone at a list, sticky note, or whiteboard and JD3 reads the tasks for you.
  • Rollover fixes: no more phantom recurring tasks, and tasks already in a past stretch-goal slot no longer resurface.
Version 2.1 April 3, 2026
  • Smarter rollovers — each unfinished task now offers four clear choices, with a bulk-apply bar and the option to schedule it for a specific day.
  • Optional task dates — give a task a date when it matters, leave it off when it doesn't.
  • Today completion haptics — finish your three and feel a little celebration (with vibration fallback on older devices).
  • Polished backlog and planning flows, plus rollover pill-button fixes.
Version 2 March 20, 2026
  • First-launch onboarding — a quick 4-slide walkthrough so new folks get the JD3 idea right away.
  • New splash screen with the bulb logo and a teal glow.
  • Brag & share card — celebrate a finished day and share it however you like.
  • Paste multiple tasks into the backlog at once — great for moving lists in from other apps.
  • Backlog screen: prominent Add task and Import File buttons.
  • Fixes to the Add-a-task button visibility, celebration banner, and Today slot counting.
Version 1 March 12, 2026

Initial release.
