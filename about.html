About

Built around one idea.

Three things done beats a hundred things half-started.

What is Just Do Three?

Just Do Three is a free iOS app for people who are tired of giant to-do lists that never get shorter. Instead of tracking everything, JD3 asks you to pick three tasks that matter most today — and do those.

That's it. No projects, no tags, no productivity theater. Just three things. Every day.

Built by an indie developer

Just Do Three is made by one person — a developer who got frustrated with their own to-do list and decided to build something better. No company, no team, no investors. Just a person who wanted a simpler way to get through the day.

The app is free because it should be. A simple daily productivity tool shouldn't cost a subscription. JD3 has no ads, no tracking, and no upsells — just the app.

If you find it useful, the best thing you can do is leave a review on the App Store. It genuinely makes a difference for an indie app.

The philosophy

Most productivity systems fail because they try to capture everything. The list grows, the backlog gets intimidating, and eventually you avoid the app entirely. Sound familiar?

Just Do Three is built on the opposite idea: ruthless simplicity. You get three slots for today. Unfinished tasks roll over. Your backlog is there when you need it, but it doesn't dominate your day.

Finishing three real things feels good!
